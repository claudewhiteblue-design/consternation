Across 55 months, a category one standard deviation more concentrated raised its prices about 1% less than its peers. The gap opens within three months and never closes: it sits below zero in 46 of 54 months. What it does not do is track the shekel, in either direction.

Why the window decides the number

One regression, one path — but the effect you report depends entirely on where you start reading it. These are the same coefficients, differenced from four different base months to July 2026.

Read fromCR3 GiantImport exposure

An earlier version of this analysis started in January 2024 and reported a positive concentration effect. That row is visible above as −0.09%, and it was positive under the older concentration measure. Nothing in the data changed; the starting point did. Any single number here is a difference between two noisy months against standard errors of 1.0 to 1.5, which is why the honest statement is about the level of the path, not about a change across a window.

Specification and limits

log Pct = αc + δt + Σt βt(CR3z×1[t]) + Σt φt(Giant×1[t]) + Σt ψt(Exposurez×1[t]) + εct

280 categories observed in all 55 months, 15,400 observations, January 2022 as reference, errors clustered by category, R² = 0.9949. CR3 is measured on 2022 and ranks the three largest named suppliers as a share of the whole category, leaving aggregation buckets in the denominator — without that, categories like בשר טחון טרי score 96 on a market where the named firms hold 8.5%.

The import control does almost nothing to the others while being strongly significant itself (F = 4.13). Restricting the panel to 2023 onward, so the 2022 concentration base no longer overlaps the outcome window, reproduces every result.

What this cannot tell you. A persistent level difference has many possible causes and this design separates none of them. Price here is a unit value, so a category whose product mix drifts toward cheaper lines shows the same pattern as one that held prices down. Concentrated categories differ from others in more than concentration. And a gap that appears within three months of the panel opening and then holds is as consistent with a fixed difference the fixed effects failed to absorb as with anything causal.
