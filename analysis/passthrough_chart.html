Israeli retail · 284 categories · 2024/01 – 2026/07

Concentrated categories kept more of the shekel's gain

The shekel appreciated 18.4% against the dollar over this window. Retail prices fell — but by less where suppliers are concentrated. A category one standard deviation higher in CR3 ends the period roughly 1.6% more expensive than it began, relative to a less concentrated peer. The gap is flat through 2024 and opens only as the currency moves.

How this was estimated

log Pct = αc + δt + Σt≠Jan24 βt · ( CR3z,c × 1[month = t] ) + εct

Unit of observation is a category-month: 284 categories observed in all 31 months, 8,804 observations. Price is the revenue-weighted standard price — revenue divided by standard quantity — on each category's fixed measurement basis, so a category's series never changes units. CR3 is the 2024 three-supplier concentration ratio, standardised across categories, and held fixed over time.

Category fixed effects absorb the level of each category's price; month fixed effects absorb everything common to all categories in a month — including the exchange rate itself. What is left in βt is purely the differential path of concentrated versus unconcentrated categories. Standard errors are clustered by category. January 2024 is the reference month, so β is zero there by construction.

The single-coefficient version replaces the month interactions with CR3_z × log(USD/ILS), giving one number — γ — for how much less pass-through a concentrated category shows. γ = −0.082 times the −0.203 log change in the rate implies +1.66%, which is what the event study finds independently at its endpoint (+1.64%).

Read γ's sign carefully. It is negative because the shekel appreciated: log(USD/ILS) fell. A negative interaction therefore means prices in concentrated categories stayed higher as the currency strengthened — less pass-through of a favourable move, not more.

What would undercut this. Price here is a unit value, so a shift in the mix of goods inside a category moves it even when no shelf price changes; concentrated categories may simply have a different mix drift. CR3 is measured over 2024, which overlaps the outcome window — a cleaner design would fix concentration on a pre-period. And 284 categories in one country over 31 months is a single macroeconomic episode: this is one appreciation, not a repeated experiment. The pre-2025 flatness is reassuring but it is not a placebo test.
