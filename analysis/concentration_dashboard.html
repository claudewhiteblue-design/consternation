CR3 named ranks the three largest identifiable suppliers as a share of the whole category, leaving bucket volume in the denominator. CR3 with buckets lets a bucket be ranked as if it were a firm — the conventional calculation, and the reason בשר טחון טרי scores in the nineties on a market where the named firms hold under a tenth. HHI sums squared shares over every supplier including buckets, so it inherits the same optimism; the DOJ bands (2500+ highly concentrated, 1500–2500 moderate) are marked on the chip.

Shares are computed on standard quantity, on each category's fixed measurement basis. A blank year means the category was not observed. 2026 covers January to July only, so its revenue is not comparable to a full year.
