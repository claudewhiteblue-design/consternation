Concentration moves prices. The five giants, on this evidence, do not.

Concentrated categories held prices ~4 points higher than unconcentrated ones as the shekel appreciated. Whether one of the five giants sits inside the category makes no detectable difference — and among concentrated categories, the ones without a giant drifted up slightly more, not less.

What this does and does not establish

The comparison is clean. Within each concentration tier, categories with and without a giant have almost identical CR3 — 93.9 vs 93.2 among the concentrated, 68.3 vs 67.7 among the rest. So the giant flag is not quietly re-measuring concentration; the four cells are separable, with 55–93 categories each.

Concentration survives; the giants do not add to it. Entering both terms together, CR3 keeps its coefficient (−0.090, p = 0.038) while the giant term is indistinguishable from zero. A triple interaction — asking whether giants matter specifically inside concentrated categories, the question posed — comes back at +0.054 with p = 0.52. Concentration appears to work through market structure rather than through the identity of these five firms.

Tnuva is the one firm with a raw p below 0.05 (+0.109, p = 0.042) — and its sign says Tnuva categories passed more of the appreciation through, not less. With five firms tested, the Bonferroni threshold is 0.01 and this does not survive (corrected p = 0.21). Treat it as a lead to check, not a finding.

This is an imprecise null, not a tight zero. The 95% interval on the giant term spans roughly ±3 percentage points of price over the window — wider than the entire CR3 effect it is being compared against (+1.66% per SD). The data rule out a large giant effect; they cannot rule out a moderate one. With 284 categories and one appreciation episode, that is the limit of what this design can say.

Specification, sample and caveats otherwise follow the pass-through analysis: log price on category and month fixed effects, standard errors clustered by category, 284 categories over 31 months. Group membership is fixed from 2024 shares, so no category switches group mid-panel. A giant counts as present at a combined quantity share of 5% or more; present at all and is the #1 supplier are shown as alternatives and agree. Price is a unit value, so category mix drift remains a confounder here as before.
